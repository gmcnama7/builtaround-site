The playbook

Plain documents Tollmark owns. Every part of the system reads from them.

Always goes to Dana Anything sent outside the company. Prices, discounts and promises. Money and legal calls. Sensitive client conversations. If it isn't in the playbook, it asks instead of guessing.
Added this month: "Offer an earlier start instead of a discount." · "Before-and-after beats tips." · "The Kowalskis prefer texts." It gets smarter about this company.

This is a working picture of the real thing: a system built in Tollmark's own Claude accounts, where every area reads the same playbook, work arrives as drafts and lists, and nothing leaves the company until a person sends it.
