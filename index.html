How it works

Three steps, and Grant does the work

  1. 1

    Grant learns your business

    One conversation about how you work, plus the documents you already have.

  2. 2

    He builds and tests it

  3. 3

    You use it. He keeps it sharp.

    Training for you and your team, then updates every month.
